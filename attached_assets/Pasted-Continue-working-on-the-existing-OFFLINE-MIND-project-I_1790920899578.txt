# OFFLINE MIND

Tagline:

"A quiet place to understand what you're going through."

OFFLINE MIND is a mental-health information, guidance, and self-help resource.

It is NOT:

* a social network
* a forum
* a community feed
* a diagnosis tool
* an AI therapist
* a medical clinic
* a psychiatric clinic
* a generic wellness SaaS product

The goal is to help someone:

Understand what they may be experiencing → try something practical → know when professional help may be useful → find real support.

# 9. REUSABLE FEELING GUIDE

Create or reuse a component such as:

src/components/content/FeelingGuide.tsx

Do not duplicate the same layout for every feeling.

Each guide should contain:

## 1. Title

Example:

"Feeling anxious"

## 2. Short introduction

Explain the experience without diagnosing.

Example style:

"Anxiety can show up as worry, tension, restlessness, or a sense that something might go wrong."

Do not say:

"You have anxiety."

---

## 3. What might be happening?

Explain possible experiences using language such as:

"You might notice..."

"Sometimes..."

"It can feel like..."

"For some people..."

Do not present symptoms as proof of a disorder.

---

## 4. What you can try right now

Give practical, low-risk suggestions.

Examples:

* slow breathing
* grounding
* moving somewhere quieter
* drinking water
* writing down thoughts
* talking to someone trusted
* taking one small next step

---

## 5. What may help over the next few days

Examples:

* maintaining a basic routine
* getting enough rest
* eating regularly when possible
* reducing overwhelming demands
* talking with someone supportive
* writing down recurring thoughts
* considering professional support if the problem continues or interferes with daily life

Do not claim these actions cure mental-health conditions.

## 6. When professional help may be useful
