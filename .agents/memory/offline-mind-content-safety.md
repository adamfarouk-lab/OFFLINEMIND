---
name: Offline Mind content safety
description: Product scope and trust constraints for mental-health content and Morocco-specific support listings.
---

OFFLINE MIND is an informational and self-help resource, not a diagnosis tool, therapist, or medical institution. Its central user journey is to help someone name what they may be feeling, understand it, try a practical step, recognize when professional help may be useful, and find real support.

Do not invent medical claims, sources, professionals, organizations, contact details, or emergency resources for Morocco. Do not present a resource as verified until its details and source have actually been checked. Use compassionate, non-judgmental, non-diagnostic language and make clear that the website does not replace professional care.

**Why:** The user established accuracy and trust as core product requirements, especially for mental-health and Morocco-specific support information.

**How to apply:** Before adding or revising mental-health guidance, professional listings, or crisis information, verify the source and keep uncertainty visible; otherwise leave the resource unpublished.
